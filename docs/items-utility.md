# Utility Items

Items that provide convenience and quality of life improvements.

### King's Recall Stone

**Description:** A consumable stone that teleports the player directly back to the overworld from the Bone Realm.

**Usage:**
- Right-click to use — teleports you to the overworld at the equivalent X/Z coordinates, above ground
- Only works inside the Bone Realm; gives an error message if used elsewhere
- Consumed on use (one-time)

**Acquisition:**
- Automatically given to every player within 25 blocks of the Skeleton King's death position

---

### Hall Locator

**Description:** Compass-like item that points to the nearest Hall of Champions

**Usage:**
- Hold in hand to see direction to Hall
- Essential for finding Hall after respawn
- Useful if you get lost far from home

**Acquisition:**
- Given to new players
- Quest reward
