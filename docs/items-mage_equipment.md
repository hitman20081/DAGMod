# Mage Equipment

### Spell Scrolls

Single-use scrolls that cast powerful spells. All scrolls require the **Mage** class to use and consume **mana** on cast. Right-click to cast; mana is only consumed if the spell succeeds.

Scrolls stack to 16. All scrolls are craftable at a crafting table.

---

#### Standard Scrolls *(Uncommon)*

| Scroll | Mana Cost | Effect | Recipe (+ 2× Paper) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heal** | 20 | Restores 3 hearts | Amethyst Powder + Glistering Melon Slice |

| **Mana Shield** | 15 | Grants Resistance II for 10 seconds | Amethyst Powder + Iron Ingot |
| **Teleport (Blink)** | 30 | Teleports to the aimed wall surface (up to 20 blocks) | Amethyst Powder + Ender Pearl |
| **Ice Wall** | 35 | Places a 5×3 wall of frosted ice 3 blocks in front of you | Quartz Powder + Packed Ice |
| **Dimensional Rift** | 30 | Teleports 15 blocks forward horizontally; works in open air (unlike Blink) | Echo Dust + Eye of Ender |

---

**Usage notes:**
- Mana is checked before casting but only consumed if the spell succeeds
- Some spells fail gracefully (e.g. Blink with no wall in sight, Gravity Well/Life Drain/Polymorph with no enemies in range) — no mana lost on failure
- Ice Wall places frosted ice which degrades naturally over time and can be broken manually
- Dimensional Rift teleports horizontally regardless of whether there's a wall; Blink requires a solid surface to land on

### Wands

Wands are reusable Mage weapons that can cast spells with mana costs and cooldowns.

**Wand Types:**
- **Apprentice Wand:** Early-game Mage weapon.
- **Adept Wand:** Mid-game Mage weapon.
- **Master Wand:** Late-game Mage weapon.

**Features:**
- Reusable, with mana costs and cooldowns for spells.
- Each wand type offers increasing power and potentially different spell effects.
- Mana cost and cooldowns are managed by the Mage class system.

**Acquisition:**
- Quest rewards
- Crafting
- Boss drops
